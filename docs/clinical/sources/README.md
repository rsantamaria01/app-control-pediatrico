## Slice 13 alpha amendment (Raul, 16 Nov 2026)

The alpha imports only plain regular-schedule name, dose number, recommended age and explicitly stated minimum interval from the archived infographic (PDF page 1) **and** manual (PDF pages 124–125 and the relevant vaccine page). Each entry carries both exact filenames, PDF page numbers and file SHA-256 through `scripts/preventive-catalog-manifest.json`; `scripts/preventive-catalog-entries.json` contains only the hand-checked facts. `pnpm exec tsx scripts/import-preventive-catalogs.ts verify` verifies original bytes, while `import` verifies them before idempotently adding immutable draft releases to the configured database. The AAP PDF is fetched from its official URL and its hash must match before importing age-only visits. `publicationDate: null` means the source does not establish an exact publication **day**; the AAP version states approval December 2024, publication February 2025, and its PDF page 1 says updated February 2025. The imported AAP entry provenance is exactly `AAP Bright Futures periodicity (ages only), pending review`; no AAP prose is copied. The 3–5 day visit also stores the literal day range rather than a rounded month approximation. No WHO or CDC developmental milestone is seeded. Doctor-authored entries are separate drafts.

**Alpha is not clinical sign-off:** every seeded row shows `Borrador - pendiente de revisión`. No due/overdue guidance, catch-up algorithm, patient advice or reminders derive from these catalogs in slice 13. The infographic's positioned labels were compared to manual table 15: BCG/HB0 at birth; rotavirus/hexavalent/pneumococcal at 2 and 4 months; hexavalent/pneumococcal/bOPV at 6; FA/SRP at 12; varicella at 15; hexavalent/bOPV/SRP at 18; DPT/bOPV/varicella at 5 years; HPV at 9. Hexavalent **contains IPV** and is not another bOPV dose (manual PDF 159–160, 163–164). Minimum PCV interval is *unknown*, not computed from 2/4/6. Excluded: maternal VSR, influenza, COVID, dT at 15 years, commercial PCV valency and any unsupported minimum interval. The schedule's ages are target ages, not an automatic eligibility rule; BCG/HB0 newborn day limits and delayed/catch-up paths are not modeled here.
