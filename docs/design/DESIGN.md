# Control Pediátrico — design specification

Status: design contract for the `feat/alpha` app, 17 November 2026. UI strings below are Spanish (`es-EC`); specification prose is English. This document consolidates the accepted visual language of the historical board with the currently implemented flows. It does not turn board sample patients, reference curves, clinical claims, legal text or unbuilt screens into production data or functionality.

## How to use this file

1. Before changing UI, read the applicable dated decision and pediatrician feedback; they override this document if amended. Then read this file for the shell, tokens, route, copy, component and state checklist. Check the actual page/component for the current API and permissions. The accepted board remains the historical visual baseline for comparable layouts, but use it to resolve missing detail or compare images, not to reintroduce superseded copy; its prototype is not authoritative.
2. Map every changed route to the screen inventory below, including phone (390×844; also test 375), tablet (1024×768), desktop (1440×900), light/dark and empty/error states. Follow `.agents/skills/design-fidelity/SKILL.md` for visual and structural verification; compare board screenshots and app captures for like-for-like states, and verify keyboard/contrast separately. Do not copy board numbers or curves.
3. A drawn but unimplemented flow is marked **future board pattern**; it must not be described to users as available. Record every difference from the historical board in **Cambios respecto al board** with what, why and the controlling guideline/decision. A current implementation difference is not approval; changes to a flow or role visibility remain **Pendiente de aprobación** until explicitly accepted. Update this specification and changelog alongside any accepted UI change.

## 1. Principles and audience

- **Clinical clarity without automatic diagnosis.** Doctors record base measurements, view verified WHO references, raw Z and provisional percentiles, and interpret results with families. A review queue is a prompt for clinician attention, not a diagnosis. No made-up LMS values, automatic trend flags, traffic-light words (`Normal`, `Vigilar`, `Riesgo`) or extrapolation outside verified age/sex/position coverage. Compare unrounded Z strictly beyond ±2/±3; equal boundaries are not outside. An unavailable reference says `No disponible`.
- **Limited sharing by design.** A guardian sees only verified linked children and the latest doctor-shared visit's date, weight, length/height and head circumference, shared advice and optional next control. Family home/detail and separate growth view never show Z, percentiles, BMI, SD labels, status colours, range assessments, diagnoses or private notes. A doctor controls visit sharing (default on); an unshared visit disappears from family projections. Current child detail also shows `Controles anteriores` for previously shared visits; this goes beyond the explicit latest-visit-only decision and needs product review, not elevation into an approved requirement. A family PDF is separately previewed and explicitly approved, not an extension of a chart permission.
- **Role-specific tasks.** DOCTOR sees all patients in this clinic, clinical file, charts and review queues. ASSISTANT handles agenda, requests, hours and verified guardian invitations with only child name, contact and link status; never charts or measurements. PATIENT (guardian) has read-only approved child information, own bookings, notices and preferences. ADMIN handles clinic configuration and draft catalog review; ADMIN alone has no clinical access, and combined privileges require an explicit DOCTOR role. Public users can sign in, accept invitations, recover access and request a first booking; never see a staff/family shell or gain family access just by verifying booking contact.
- **Honest status.** Bookings remain pending until staff approve; no fake confirmation. Draft source entries are facts with per-entry provenance and `Borrador - pendiente de revisión`, never copied third-party prose, invented intervals or definitive advice. Empty milestones stay empty until sourced or entered by a doctor. Unconfigured channels say `No configurado`; legal/privacy copy stays visibly draft pending counsel, with no invented response or retention deadlines. Source/rights checks are pilot gates, not reasons to invent alpha content. Keep saved-record state, invitation delivery, booking approval and report approval separate; a transient toast cannot stand in for any of these.
- **One clinic per deployment.** Only clinic logo and primary palette can change; neutral, Z display and chart colours are fixed. Offline shows no authenticated HTML or cached clinical records; `/api/**` is NetworkOnly.

All authenticated staff use `UDashboardGroup` + `UDashboardSidebar` + `UDashboardPanel` at ≥1024px. **Decidido por Raul 2026-11-17:** A user with any staff role (DOCTOR, ASSISTANT, ADMIN) always has the staff shell, even when also PATIENT; guardian-only users have the `UHeader` shell. ## 5. Screen and page patterns

For each screen, read the ordered regions left-to-right/top-to-bottom. Unless otherwise noted: desktop content sits inside the staff sidebar/top bar or guardian header with responsive cards; tablet keeps the desktop sidebar (often icon collapsed) while multi-column content may stack; phone stacks content above bottom tabs, scrolls horizontal dense lists/tables, uses labelled back controls and safe-area-aware fixed actions. Keep the page's primary task and patient identity/date visible before secondary drill-downs; put detailed measurements in the file/chart rather than a crowded home or search row. At 375px, 200% zoom and landscape, allow reflow without horizontal page scrolling; only genuinely tabular/chart content may scroll within a named region. Text may wrap and cards grow; never clip clinical values or put the final input/action behind a virtual keyboard, sticky bar or bottom tab. Dark mode retains the same region order and replaces canvas/card/border/text tokens as above; never darken print PDFs. A sample status or measurement in an artboard is not live clinical content.

## 7. Accessibility and language

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA: normal text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 and essential non-text (controls, meaningful icons, focus indicators and distinguishable chart lines) ≥3:1 against adjacent colours in **both** themes; calculate on actual composited backgrounds, not token swatches alone. Test each allowed clinic primary/logo and all interactive/disabled/selected states. Decorative card dividers need not meet 3:1 unless they define a control boundary. The 2px/2px-offset `:focus-visible` outline must remain perceivable on every surface; if the configurable primary fails 3:1, use a contrast-safe focus treatment instead of silently keeping the failing brand hue. In forced-colours mode preserve outlines, borders, text and icons without relying on shadows or fills.
- WCAG 2.2 AA target minimum is 24×24 CSS px or sufficient 24px separation; this product retains the stronger **44×44px** touch-target rule on all touch-capable layouts, including calendar dates, slots, chevrons, icon buttons and tabs. Do not shrink the hit area just to match an artboard. Focus must never be **fully** hidden by sticky headers, mobile tabs, overlays or the virtual keyboard (2.4.11 AA); aim for fully visible focus where practical. Provide a visible skip-to-content link, one main landmark, distinguishable navigation landmarks, descriptive route titles and a logical heading/tab order. Name icon-only actions, hide decorative icons from assistive technology and announce selected/expanded state via the native control or matching ARIA (`aria-current="page"` for current route; proper selected tab state for tabs, not `aria-pressed` on every tab). Colour is never the only signal of a Z, source or booking state.
- Forms keep visible labels, required-state, contextual hints and errors linked to their fields; after failed submit focus the linked error summary (or first invalid field for a short form). Validation appears after interaction/submit, not on first render or each keystroke; server errors retain input and separate saved-record state from invite/send outcome. Permit username/password managers, paste, code paste and backtracking through booking steps without losing entered data. Calendar/slots/agenda, radio cards, switches, modal controls and chart zoom work by keyboard; no hover-, drag- or swipe-only action. Modal focus enters the dialog, cannot leak into the background and returns to its trigger on close. Keep search navigable by keyboard (`Ctrl K` on doctor desktop). Charts have a contextual description, accessible table and keyboard zoom so critical values are not tooltip-only; label sex neutrally, never pink/blue.
- Test 375/390px phone (portrait and landscape), 1024px tablet, 1440px desktop, 200% zoom and 320 CSS px reflow, light/dark, increased text, reduced motion and forced-colours, including auth/booking, empty/error/offline, PDF preview and overlays. At zoom and with the on-screen keyboard open, the last field, action and focus remain visible; reduced motion must not hide feedback or affect navigation. Test keyboard and a screen reader as well as automated contrast checks; screenshots alone do not certify WCAG.
- UI tone: clear, calm, precise, respectful; no premature reassurance, diagnosis, legal promise or fake delivery. Use Spanish labels exactly as specified; product copy uses `usted` for public/family instructions where appropriate and `tu pediatra` in the approved family heading. Prefer action + object (`Registrar dosis`, `Guardar visita`, `Ver agenda completa`) and honest states (`Pendiente de aprobación`, `No configurado`, `No disponible`). Clinical names, percentile thresholds, review criteria and family wording remain provisional until pediatrician review; draft legal copy is visibly `Borrador · pendiente de revisión legal` and does not claim the board's illustrative `15 días` or `5 años` as law.
- Locale `es-EC`, clinic-local time (default `America/Guayaquil`) for appointment display, unambiguous short dates (`10 nov 2026`) and 24-hour times (`10:30`); machine date in URLs is `YYYY-MM-DD`. Decimal comma, tabular numerals and true minus U+2212: `8,20 kg`, `76,4 cm`, `Z −1,39`; units `kg`, `cm`, `kg/m²`, months/years as applicable. Do not round before comparing clinical thresholds. Names/source titles are not translated into invented clinical guidance.
