# Control Pediátrico: first-release design

Status: planning baseline for a real-data alpha; six clinical choices below are provisional, to be reviewed with the pediatrician at the alpha demo. Date: 2026-11-12. This document records Raul's choices relayed by Claude and compatible input from the pediatrician's verbatim `docs/clinical/2026-11-10-pediatrician-data-requirements.md` (sent 2026-11-10 16:24). Accepted clinical and UI decisions in `docs/decisions/2026-11-10-ui-and-clinical.md` override older briefs and illustrative board data where they differ; conflicts and new scope are listed below for confirmation. The board in `docs/design/` is the primary visual reference; its prototype is secondary.

## Intent and outcome

Ship the accepted board as one first release for a private pediatric practice in Guayaquil: doctors record visits and explain WHO growth results; guardians see only their own children's information, appointments and recommendations; assistants run the agenda without clinical access; administrators configure and audit the clinic. Build in independently testable vertical slices, but do not call a partial slice the first release. The initial launch is a controlled pilot behind a production-ready deployment boundary. Raul explicitly decided that the pilot may use real identifiable children's records and accept public bookings while privacy/consent/LOPDP texts are visibly marked as drafts; he accepts the risk of that choice. Lawyer-approved legal texts remain a gate before a broader public launch. UI copy is Spanish (`es-EC`); identifiers and implementation documentation are English.

### Goals, non-goals and anti-goals

- **Goals:** The alpha itself includes the full board, and every visible screen must work end to end:  username/password sign-in, recovery and invitation flows; doctor dashboard, patients, visits, clinical notes and advice; WHO growth charts and Z displays; parent portal; day/week/month agenda and opening hours; manually approved public booking; verified vaccine and development catalogs and records; checkup schedule and screenings; PDF growth report; email, WhatsApp and in-app reminders/notifications; administration, privacy/data-request workflow, audit, accessible light/dark PWA and safe offline state.
- **Not in release one:** WHO 2007 growth results for ages 5–19, cross-clinic accounts, a shared database serving multiple clinics, automatic booking approval, or legally final LOPDP copy. Fenton curves are out of the alpha provisionally (review with pediatrician at alpha demo); keep the model extensible for later Fenton and postmenstrual-age support. AAP controls through age 21 are in release one, independent of growth-reference coverage. Support for WHO 2007 is planned without schema replacement.
- **Never:** clinical advice from mock/unchecked reference data; a Z derived from an unsupported age or sex/table; parent access from booking-email verification alone; clinical data exposed to an assistant or another family's guardian; private clinical notes in parent output or PDF; secrets in source or client; cached clinical API/pages in the service worker; promises of legal deadlines unsupported by counsel.

## Approaches considered

1. **Chosen: independent deployment and database per clinic, one Nuxt app with Nitro routes.** One account belongs to that deployment; the clinic's hostname serves its public booking, identity and branding. PostgreSQL owns records and durable delivery intent; Valkey runs jobs; RustFS holds files. This fits the existing single-app stack and the November white-label decision without introducing clinic-scoped queries.
2. **Rejected: shared multi-tenant deployment with clinic identifiers.** Would require tenant-isolation policies, clinic memberships and hostname mapping not wanted by Raul and would expand security surface.
3. **Rejected: separate booking/clinical applications or synchronous notification delivery.** Adds integration boundaries before any useful slice and risks blocking bookings on providers. Keep one app, queue outbound work.

## Architecture and data model outline

- Each installation has its own app, PostgreSQL, Valkey and RustFS. A single clinic-settings record holds display name, contact, logo reference, primary color, opening hours, timezone (default Ecuador), and configurable notification policies. Only logo and primary palette vary; Z, reference curves, patient line and neutral colors do not. Bootstrap an administrator without open self-registration. Admin/doctor combined duties must be explicitly authorized by assigned roles, never inferred from a job title.
- Server routes enforce role and relationship authorization for every read/write; hiding a link is not access control. Better Auth username + email/password sign-in for every role, invited-only account setup (P6 superseded 12 Nov 2026). Lowercase case-insensitive unique usernames of 3–32 `[a-z0-9._-]`; passwords minimum 10 characters, no forced composition rules, reject a local common/breached-password list, default Better Auth hashing and UI strength hint. Valkey rate limits by username and IP with progressive delay/temporary lockout and generic error `Usuario o contraseña incorrectos`; audit failures. Email is for invitations and password recovery only: recovery by username/email issues a single-use 10-minute code/link, with an account/delivery-independent response, cooldown, redacted structured failure logging, durable delivery-failure record and admin-readable email-provider readiness (`No configurado`/failing). An admin may force a fresh reset link. Changing password revokes other sessions. Bootstrap first ADMIN by CLI with username/email and one-time setup link, no password in arguments/env/logs. Staff/admin/assistant sessions last 12 hours and guardians 30 days. Seven-day invitation token is bound to the intended guardian/child; DOCTOR or ASSISTANT verifies guardianship, grants the relationship and explicitly sends the invite (ordinarily the assistant does this). The assistant sees only invite-needed child name, guardian contact and link status, never the clinical file. Booking contact verification alone only validates contact, never portal access. Define revocation and audit for guardianship changes. Rate-limit sign-in by username/IP and public recovery, booking and invite endpoints; protect against enumeration and repeated submissions; use secure cookies and narrowly trusted proxy headers behind external TLS.
- Candidate tables/entities (names are outline, not final migrations): users, credentials, sessions, single-use recovery/setup tokens and invitations, staff roles, clinic settings, patients, guardian relationships and verification/grant events, visits, patient birth/perinatal observations (gestational weeks/days, birth weight, length and head circumference), base measurements (date, weight, measured length/height with explicit recumbent/standing position, head circumference, chronological/corrected age derivation, reference used per calculation), immutable per-measurement result snapshots (Z, percentile, classification, reference source/version and age basis, with any recalculation recorded as a separate identified result), private notes, parent-visible recommendations and indications, doctor-approved family-report snapshots, appointments, availability/blocked dates, booking requests and approval events, checkup templates and overrides, catalog releases and entries, vaccine doses/catch-up events, milestone assessments and screenings, notification preferences/rules, notification outbox/delivery attempts, consent/legal-version acknowledgments and data requests, audit events and file metadata. Derive BMI and Z rather than using user-entered derived values. Keep explicit boundary between private notes and report/shareable fields.
- Every clinical reference dataset/catalog has source, version and publication/effective/update date, plus review/activation history; growth imports also retain file hash and import timestamp. WHO reference tables have versioned dataset/source provenance (official WHO publication, source file hash, import timestamp, age range, sex, indicator, measurement position, x-axis/unit) and LMS/SD rows keyed by dataset + indicator + sex + x. WHO 2006 originals are authoritative; if obtaining numerical files through the CDC WHO-data-files index, verify that they are the WHO tables and preserve their source/license. Use official WHO Anthro for independent result validation (AnthroPlus for WHO 2007 when added). Provisionally show percentiles next to Z for clinicians, families and approved PDFs as the pediatrician's source requests; review placement and wording with her at the alpha demo. The seven SD curves remain unchanged. Import validated, official 2006 data for 0–5 first (including head circumference and separate length/height and weight-for-length/height references); handle the 24-month transition and 0.7 cm adjustment with tests. Reserve dataset/version/age/indicator flexibility for 2007 5–19 data, including weight-for-age only through 10 years. A missing reference returns an explicit unavailable state; older patients may be recorded but must not get 2006-derived Z or curves. Keep `correctZ` disjoint-regression behavior with pinned tests, and replace old traffic-light `zScoreBand` only after indicator-specific WHO names and exact threshold tests exist. Corrected age applies through 24 months, or individually through 36 months for extreme preterm babies. Record gestational age, chronological age and corrected age derivation; preserve reference and age basis used for each result. Provisionally use corrected age on WHO curves in the alpha without Fenton; review with the pediatrician at the alpha demo. Reserve postmenstrual-age and Fenton source/transition fields for later without inventing a Fenton result now.
- Clinical catalogs: versioned source/review metadata and activation per deployment. In the **alpha**, seed WHO six motor windows, CDC age/domain checklists, AAP newborn–21 controls, development screening at 9/18/30 months, autism screening at 18/24 months and MSP vaccines from original official MSP documents; each seeded entry is explicitly `DRAFT_PENDING_PEDIATRICIAN_REVIEW` with source/version/date and visible `Borrador · pendiente de revisión pediátrica` badge. Alpha workflows may calculate and display draft due items only when unmistakably labelled as unreviewed—not as definitive clinical guidance. In the real release, only reviewed active entries produce due/overdue guidance. The pediatrician supplies WHO six motor acquisition windows (sitting unsupported 3.8–9.2 months; standing assisted 4.8–11.4; crawling on all fours 5.2–13.5; walking assisted 6.0–13.7; standing alone 6.9–16.9; walking alone 8.2–17.6) and CDC age/domain checklists for gross/fine motor, language/communication, cognition and social/emotional development. Import original dated/versioned source files and verify the six windows before the draft alpha seed; pediatrician reviews/activates them for definitive release guidance. Crawling is not a prerequisite to walking; windows never imply an automatic developmental-delay diagnosis. Store assessed/not achieved/not assessed status, age of acquisition and private observations. Screening reminders: general development at 9/18/30 months; autism at 18/24 months; distinguish reminder from an applied test and record its instrument and result. Keep the accepted M-CHAT reminder requirement, but confirm its exact instrument/age mapping with the pediatrician. Store the reviewed baseline separately from clinic overrides and recorded doses/assessments; allow clinic edits, private vaccines and catch-up without silently rewriting provenance. For MSP vaccines, model biological name, diseases prevented, recommended/min/max ages, dose count/number, minimum interval, boosters, route, special indications/contraindications, catch-up rules and effective date. Dose records include administration date, institution (including external), manufacturer, batch, site and route. Derive due/overdue only from verified official MSP schedule and immunization manual, never from a calendar illustration alone. A configured checkup schedule follows accepted AAP ages and permits extras; the new pediatrician list specifically names newborn, 3–5 days, 1/2/4/6/9/12/15/18/24/30 months, 3/4/5 years and annually 6–21. Raul confirmed the entire newborn-to-21 AAP schedule is in release one, independent of unavailable 5–19 WHO growth Z. Calculate next/pending/late checkups without re-alerting completed ones, and let the doctor individualize schedules for preterm/chronic patients and add extraordinary visits. Do not invent a growth-trend criterion or MSP doses/intervals: the MSP page links a 2026 infographic and manual, but extract and reconcile official documents before seeding; if any required manual cannot be obtained or parsed, flag the catalog as blocked and ask Raul for the original rather than synthesizing rules.
- Appointments and booking requests have explicit pending/approved/declined/cancelled states and transactional capacity checks to prevent double-booking; doctor/assistant approves manually. Store local clinic timezone alongside instants for displayed scheduling. Generate durable delivery intents when domain transactions commit; enqueue through an outbox/worker so Valkey failure or provider delay does not hold up bookings. Idempotency keys and attempt history prevent duplicate email/WhatsApp/in-app notifications; retries have bounded backoff, dead-letter/operational visibility and per-channel opt-in. Support Meta Cloud API and Twilio; admin-managed credentials encrypted at rest, environment override wins and secrets are never echoed after saving. The alpha must operate without outbound credentials: in-app messages always work; email and WhatsApp channels display `No configurado` and do not claim delivery. Admin UI configures providers/secrets with environment override; working outbound delivery requires credentials and approved templates. Invites and recovery requests return generic account/delivery-independent acknowledgments, never `enviado`; recovery copy is `Si la cuenta existe, enviaremos instrucciones al correo registrado`. Invitation delivery status is shown only to authorized staff, not publicly; persist failures and mark provider `No configurado`/failing for admins. Use SMTP env (`SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS`, `SMTP_FROM`, `SMTP_SECURE`) and a local development/test transport until Raul supplies real credentials. Never return the code/token in public responses or logs.
- PDF comes from a constrained shareable projection: patient identity, date, latest measurements/results, WHO curves, authorized clinical results, recommendations, medical indications, vaccines, development, screenings and next control; **never** private notes, internal differential diagnoses, confidential administrative observations or other unapproved fields. The DOCTOR previews and explicitly approves each report's contents before it becomes available to the family; preserve the approved snapshot, approving doctor and time for audit and prevent later private-data changes from leaking into it. Serve on authorization, avoid public URLs and treat RustFS objects as private. Prefer a print/PDF rendering that matches three A4 board pages and independently test exclusion of private fields. Provisionally include percentiles beside Z on the approved PDF and review this with the pediatrician at the alpha demo.
- Audit clinical reads by doctors, writes/changes, access grants, invites, booking approvals, report generation, exports, role/config changes and data requests, with actor, action, subject, time and outcome; do not copy full clinical contents or secrets into logs. Retention periods and legal response deadlines await counsel; the UI must not assert the board's illustrative numbers as legal fact.

## Access matrix (server enforced)

| Action | ADMIN | DOCTOR | ASSISTANT | PATIENT/guardian | Public |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clinic branding/users/roles, provider setup, audit and data-request administration | Yes | Only if also ADMIN | No | Own data request only | No |
| Patient file, visits, measurements, WHO Z/curves, private notes | Only if also DOCTOR; admin duties do not imply chart access | All patients of this clinic; log reads | No | Own children: approved shareable subset, no private notes | No |
| Guardian verification, relationship grant and portal invite | Account administration, no chart access by role alone | Verify, grant and invite | Verify, grant and invite; child name/contact/link status only | No | No |
| Agenda, availability, booking review/approval | Admin if assigned staff permission | Yes | Yes, schedule/contact data only | Own appointments/request | Submit and verify own request only |
| Vaccines, development, assessments and recommendations | Only if also DOCTOR | Read/write | No | Own children: published/shareable subset | No |
| Parent-safe report/PDF | Only if also DOCTOR | Generate and access | No | Own children | No |
| Notification preferences | Clinic policy as ADMIN | Own preferences | Own preferences | Own channels | Booking-contact consent only |

Authorize via a server-side capability map, explicit roles and patient-guardian relationships; ADMIN does not automatically confer clinical read access. Assistant agenda payloads must not include measurements, Z, notes or a patient-file link. Audit views are admin-only. Assistant verification access is explicitly limited to child name, guardian contact and invite/link status; no measurements, Z, medical history, notes or patient-file navigation.

## UI and accessibility refinements accepted for implementation

Preserve the board's structure, light/dark themes and only-primary/logo white-labeling, but correct obsolete `Normal`/`Riesgo` phone chips and any similar labels to the accepted WHO-name/Z rules. Both doctor and parent charts show seven official WHO SD curves with end labels; parent copy is plain-language, with value + `dentro / fuera del rango esperado`, and outside ±2 adds the doctor's-title reassurance. Record seven parent curves as a change from the parent artboard requiring pediatrician confirmation but **not** a scope blocker. Keep C10 family wording centralized and add percentiles beside Z provisionally for the alpha demo. Use decimal comma, U+2212 and tabular numerals. Reflow 10px chart labels on narrow phones, retain always-accessible table and keyboard zoom controls, theme-match legends to curves, check contrast in both themes. Make phone content scroll beneath safe-area-aware fixed bars and save actions; do not clip long forms. Replace obsolete OTP-sign-in artboards with username/password, invitation setup and recovery forms; turn decorative booking/agenda/recovery-code/switch mockups into semantic, keyboard-operable controls with visible focus, error summary/field errors, selected state, accessible names and pasteable OTP. Recheck chart/board at 375, 1024 and 1440 px, dark and light, reduced motion and zoom. Preserve no-offline-clinical-data screen and verify service worker never caches authenticated HTML or `/api/**`. Legal copy remains explicitly draft, without unverified retention/response deadlines. Board screenshots could not be visually inspected in the brainstorming environment; a rendered review and contrast measurements are implementation verification tasks.

## Provisional clinical choices for alpha (pediatrician reviews at demo)

Raul decided to build a real-data alpha and gather the pediatrician's feedback by showing working screens rather than sending questions now. Items 1–4 and 7–8 are **provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo**; record her changes before full first-release planning. Items 5–6 are resolved by Raul. No automatic diagnosis or unverified source data.

1. **Family wording — provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo:** Follow C10 `dentro / fuera del rango esperado` plus Z and doctor reassurance outside ±2 for now. Keep all family range copy behind a single copy/config point so wording can change without reworking clinical logic.
2. **Percentiles — provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo:** Display them beside Z for doctors and families and in each doctor-approved PDF; derive from the same verified reference and retain seven WHO SD curves.
3. **Classification names — provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo:** Add `talla alta para la edad` (height-for-age Z > +2) and `microcefalia severa` (head circumference Z < −3) from her source; retain C3 `riesgo de sobrepeso` for now. Keep names, severity ordering and thresholds in one table-driven module with unit tests; do not display WHO clinical names automatically to families. WHO 2007 BMI classifications remain later scope.
4. **Preterm Fenton — provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo:** No Fenton in alpha; preterm results use corrected age on WHO curves under C1. Keep reference/age basis extensible to support Fenton and postmenstrual age after reviewing version, license and transition with her.
5. **Historical results — resolved by Raul:** Persist an immutable snapshot per measurement with Z, percentile, classification, reference source/version and age basis used. A recalculation produces a separate identified result; it does not overwrite history. Approved family PDF snapshots remain reproducible. BMI/Z are calculated from base measurements, never manually entered.
6. **AAP schedule — resolved by Raul:** Include newborn, 3–5 days, 1/2/4/6/9/12/15/18/24/30 months, ages 3/4/5 and annual controls through 21 in release one. Checkup scheduling is independent of WHO growth-reference availability; growth Z for ages 5–19 remains unavailable until validated WHO 2007 data is added.
7. **Exactly ±2/±3 — provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo:** Apply strict `<`/`>` to classification and tones as written in both sources; exactly −2.00 is not `talla baja`. Test equality and immediately adjacent values for every indicator and age branch; do not classify by rounded display Z.
8. **Trend criterion — provisional, review with pediatrician at alpha demo:** No automatic trend flag in alpha; DOCTOR can mark trend manually, with attribution/audit. No unsupported automatic clinical alert. Revisit a testable longitudinal criterion after the demo.
